Information We Collect

Silent Toggle Widget does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Permissions Used

The app requires certain Android permissions only to provide its core functionality.

  • Notification Policy Access (Do Not Disturb Access)
    Required to enable or disable Do Not Disturb mode when requested by the user.
  • Alarm & Schedule Permission
    Used to automatically switch between Silent, Vibrate, Normal, or DND modes according to your configured schedules.
  • Foreground Service (if applicable)
    Used to ensure scheduled sound profile changes work reliably in the background.
  • Vibration Permission
    Used only for vibration-related functionality.

These permissions are used only on your device and are never used to collect personal information.

Widget Functionality

The Home Screen Widget allows quick switching between:

  • Silent Mode
  • Vibrate Mode
  • Normal Mode
  • Do Not Disturb (DND)

All widget operations are performed locally on your device.
