Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

Thank you for using Daily Affirmation. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

Daily Affirmation does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

We do not collect:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages
  • Payment Information
  • Any Personal Documents

Local Storage

To provide a better user experience, the app stores some settings locally on your device only. These may include:

  • Reminder Settings
  • Notification Time
  • Daily Affirmation Progress
  • Widget Preferences
  • App Preferences

This information never leaves your device.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the application may schedule local notifications to remind you to read your daily affirmation. These notifications are generated locally on your device and are not sent through our servers.

Internet Access

Daily Affirmation does not require an internet connection for its core features. The application works offline for reading affirmations, widgets, and reminders.

Third-Party Services

Daily Affirmation does not use third-party services that collect personal information.

If future versions include services such as Google Play Services, Firebase, Analytics, Crash Reporting, Advertising, or Cloud Synchronization, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

Daily Affirmation is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Data Security

Since your data remains stored locally on your device, you have full control over your information. We do not have access to your local data.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Enable or disable reminders at any time.
  • Clear application data from your device settings.
  • Uninstall the application to remove all locally stored data.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
