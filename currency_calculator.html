Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Currency Calculator ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. Currency Calculator is designed to provide currency conversion, calculation, exchange-rate information, and home-screen widget functionality.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The App may store certain settings locally on your device, such as:

  • Selected currencies
  • Preferred currency
  • Calculator preferences
  • Widget configuration
  • Other app preferences required for the App to function

This information is stored locally on your device and is not intended to identify you personally.

2. Currency Exchange Rates

The App may obtain currency exchange-rate information from third-party exchange-rate or data providers.

When exchange rates are requested, information necessary to obtain the requested exchange-rate data may be transmitted to the relevant service provider.

We do not use your currency calculations to identify you personally.

Exchange rates may change frequently, and the rates displayed by the App may differ from rates offered by banks, financial institutions, payment providers, or currency exchanges.

3. Currency Calculator

The App allows you to enter amounts and calculate conversions between different currencies.

Amounts entered into the calculator are used to perform the requested calculation. We do not intentionally collect or store these amounts as personal information.

You should not use the App as a substitute for professional financial advice.

4. Home-Screen Widget

The App provides currency-related information and conversion functionality through a home-screen widget.

The widget may store selected currencies and other configuration information locally on your device so that it can display the information you have selected.

5. Internet Access

The App may use an internet connection to retrieve current or updated currency exchange-rate information.

Internet access may also be required for certain third-party services used to provide exchange-rate data or other App functionality.

6. Advertising and Analytics

If the App contains advertising or analytics services, those services may collect certain information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Such information may include device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, or other technical information depending on the services integrated into the App.

If advertising or analytics services are not enabled in your version of the App, no such information is collected by those services.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide exchange-rate data, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other technical functionality.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party services used by the App.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where information is transmitted over the internet, appropriate security measures such as encrypted connections may be used where supported by the relevant service.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not require a user account, we generally do not maintain an account containing your personal information.

App preferences stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

Information processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to their respective policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Currency Calculator is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
